📖 Una visita al dentista

🇪🇸 Español

Hace una semana, a Luis le empezó a doler una muela cada vez que tomaba algo frío. Al principio no quería ir al dentista porque le daba mucho miedo, pero su hermana Paola le dijo: "Si esperas más, el dolor va a ser peor. Yo te acompaño."

El sábado llegaron al consultorio y se sentaron en la sala de espera. Luis estaba muy nervioso y no dejaba de mover las piernas. Por fin, la doctora Ramírez, que es muy amable, lo llamó.

Después de revisarle la boca, la doctora le explicó que tenía una caries pequeña. "No se preocupe. Le voy a poner anestesia y casi no va a sentir nada", dijo. Luego le hizo un empaste y también le quitó el sarro de los dientes. Luis tenía la mejilla dormida y no podía hablar bien.

Antes de salir, la doctora le recomendó cepillarse los dientes después de cada comida y usar hilo dental todas las noches. "Vuelva en seis meses", le dijo. Luis sonrió y contestó: "¡Qué fácil fue! La próxima vez no voy a esperar tanto."

🇬🇧 English Translation

A week ago, Luis started getting a toothache every time he drank something cold. At first he didn't want to go to the dentist because he was very scared, but his sister Paola told him: "If you wait longer, the pain is going to get worse. I'll go with you."

On Saturday they arrived at the office and sat down in the waiting room. Luis was very nervous and kept moving his legs. Finally, Dr. Ramírez, who is very kind, called him in.

After examining his mouth, the doctor explained that he had a small cavity. "Don't worry. I'm going to give you anesthesia and you'll hardly feel anything," she said. Then she gave him a filling and also removed the tartar from his teeth. Luis's cheek was numb and he couldn't speak well.

Before leaving, the doctor recommended that he brush his teeth after every meal and use dental floss every night. "Come back in six months," she told him. Luis smiled and answered: "That was so easy! Next time I'm not going to wait so long."
