📖 La fisioterapia de Andrés

🇪🇸 Español

Andrés se lesionó la rodilla mientras jugaba futbol con sus amigos el domingo pasado. Al principio pensó que solo era un golpe sin importancia, pero al día siguiente le dolía tanto que casi no podía caminar. Su médico le recomendó que fuera a fisioterapia para recuperarse bien antes de volver a entrenar.

El lunes por la mañana, Andrés llegó al consultorio nervioso porque nunca había estado en un lugar así. La fisioterapeuta, una mujer muy paciente llamada Karla, le explicó que la rehabilitación tomaría varias semanas y que era importante que siguiera cada indicación con disciplina. Le pidió que se acostara sobre la camilla y comenzó a mover su pierna con mucho cuidado.

Durante las primeras sesiones, Andrés sintió una molestia constante y dudaba de que fuera a mejorar pronto. Sin embargo, Karla le aseguró que eso era normal y que, poco a poco, notaría los avances. Le enseñó ejercicios sencillos para hacer en casa, como estirar la pierna y fortalecer los músculos alrededor de la rodilla.

Después de un mes de terapia constante, Andrés por fin pudo subir las escaleras sin dolor. Cuando volvió a la cancha con sus amigos, se sorprendió de lo fuerte que se sentía. "Si hubiera dejado la rehabilitación a medias, seguramente me habría lastimado otra vez", le comentó a Karla en su última cita, muy agradecido por su paciencia.

🇬🇧 English Translation

Andrés hurt his knee while playing soccer with his friends last Sunday. At first he thought it was just a minor bump, but the next day it hurt so much that he could barely walk. His doctor recommended that he go to physical therapy to recover properly before going back to training.

On Monday morning, Andrés arrived at the office nervous because he had never been in a place like that before. The physical therapist, a very patient woman named Karla, explained that the rehabilitation would take several weeks and that it was important for him to follow every instruction with discipline. She asked him to lie down on the table and began to move his leg very carefully.

During the first sessions, Andrés felt constant discomfort and doubted he would improve soon. However, Karla assured him that this was normal and that, little by little, he would notice progress. She taught him simple exercises to do at home, like stretching his leg and strengthening the muscles around his knee.

After a month of steady therapy, Andrés was finally able to climb stairs without pain. When he went back to the field with his friends, he was surprised at how strong he felt. "If I had given up on the rehabilitation halfway through, I probably would have hurt myself again," he told Karla at his last appointment, very grateful for her patience.
