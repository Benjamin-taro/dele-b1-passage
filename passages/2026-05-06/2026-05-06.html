📖 El torneo de videojuegos

🇪🇸 Español

Andrés nunca pensó que participar en un torneo de videojuegos le traería tantas amistades. Desde pequeño le gustaba pasar las tardes frente a la pantalla, pero siempre lo hacía solo en su cuarto. Todo cambió cuando su prima le recomendó un evento que organizaba un local de videojuegos en su colonia.

La noche del torneo, el local estaba lleno de jóvenes de diferentes edades. Andrés llegó un poco tarde y, al no encontrar lugar, se sentó junto a un chico que jugaba muy concentrado. Después de un rato, los dos comenzaron a platicar sobre estrategias y, sin darse cuenta, decidieron formar un equipo.

El torneo duró tres horas. Aunque no ganaron el primer lugar, Andrés y su nuevo amigo, Tomás, llegaron hasta la semifinal, lo que les pareció todo un logro. Al terminar, el dueño del local explicó que el torneo se repite cada mes y que siempre hay categorías para jugadores principiantes y avanzados.

Andrés regresó a casa con muchas ganas de practicar. Esa noche le contó a su mamá lo que había pasado. Ella, que antes pensaba que los videojuegos eran una pérdida de tiempo, escuchó emocionada cómo su hijo hablaba de trabajo en equipo y de nuevas amistades.

🇬🇧 English Translation

Andrés never thought that taking part in a video game tournament would bring him so many friends. Since he was young, he liked spending his afternoons in front of the screen, but he always did it alone in his room. Everything changed when his cousin recommended an event organized by a video game venue in their neighborhood.

On the night of the tournament, the venue was full of young people of different ages. Andrés arrived a little late and, unable to find a spot, sat down next to a boy who was playing very focused. After a while, the two of them started chatting about strategies and, without realizing it, decided to form a team.

The tournament lasted three hours. Although they didn't win first place, Andrés and his new friend Tomás made it to the semifinals, which they both considered a real achievement. When it was over, the owner of the venue explained that the tournament takes place every month and that there are always categories for beginner and advanced players.

Andrés went home eager to practice more. That night he told his mom what had happened. She, who had previously thought that video games were a waste of time, listened with excitement as her son talked about teamwork and new friendships.
